THE ORIGIN STORY

    


    

      
origin_story.txt — read only

      

        
> Every generation of ape gets the tools it deserves.

        
> First there was a phone. Small screen, big thumbs, zero attention span. He scrolled. He liked. He aped. It worked — for a while. That ape had a name: $FONE.

        
> Then, one night, deep in the stacks of a library nobody visits anymore, he found something better. A desk. A real one. Beige case, humming fan, a green cursor blinking like it had been waiting for him.

        
> He sat down. He has not stood up since.

        
> He doesn't scroll anymore — he runs a book. His own book, held on-chain, in an account only he can own. Every trade gets written into it, one line at a time, the old-fashioned way.

        
> Nobody taught him OTC. Nobody taught him to type. He just found the desk and refused to leave.

        
> This is $DESK.█
